# 09 — launch subcommand

**What to build:** `python3 run.py launch` walks the whole launch through
in one command: the dirty-tree gate, per-piece card verification (reject
the whole launch if even one card isn't FREE), starting tmux, verifying
liveness for 30 seconds (session exists, the log has output, no
traceback; on failure, no registration and no rollback), the three
registrations, and printing the monitoring entry point. A job marked
shardable given multiple pieces automatically gets shard indices
injected; a job not marked shardable given multiple pieces is flatly
rejected. The session name, ledger name, record, and log name are all
generated by the program from the same run_id. A one-off command outside
the registry goes through the --cmd escape hatch, with registration still
happening as usual. --dry-run only prints the command and never touches
registration. Steps follow Task 11 of the implementation plan.
