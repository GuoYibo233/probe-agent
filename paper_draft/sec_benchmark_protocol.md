## Setup

**Episode streams.** The unit of evaluation is a *run* $r = (\sigma, s)$: an instantiated task stream $\sigma$ of $K$ episodes together with a random seed $s$ [TODO: fix $K$ and the number of runs per cell once the full matrix is scheduled]. Every run is executed twice under identical conditions — a *mem* arm, in which the subject's memory mechanism is active, and a *nomem* arm, in which memory writes and reads are disabled — so that all comparisons are paired at the level of (run, episode index, task instance). Episode $k$ of run $r$ yields an outcome $u_{r,k} \in [0,1]$ (binary success for embodied tasks; soft accuracy for QA) and a cost $c_{r,k} > 0$. The canonical cost currency is total tokens, $c = \text{tok}_{\text{in}} + \text{tok}_{\text{out}}$; wall-clock time is reported only within a single hardware configuration and never aggregated across accelerator types. Each episode additionally carries a status flag $\text{status} \in \{\text{ok}, \text{degen}, \text{forced}\}$, and all headline metrics are computed on ok episodes only (§ Aggregation).

**Frozen-backbone protocol.** All subjects share the same frozen LLM backbone; no gradient updates to backbone weights are permitted at any point. What varies across subjects is exclusively the memory mechanism: what is written after each episode, what is retrieved before and during each episode, and how retrieved content enters the context. This isolates the quantity the benchmark is designed to measure — the marginal value of experience reuse — from generic model-capability differences. Backbones: Qwen3-8B and Qwen3.5-4B [TODO: confirm final backbone list; a non-Qwen point is planned but not yet run].

**Similarity levels.** Each stream is drawn from one of five levels that control the relation between an episode and the subject's prior experience:

- **L0 (unrelated):** inter-task similarity below 0.3; retrieval should not fire, and any performance delta on L0 measures the indiscriminate overhead of the memory mechanism itself.
- **L1 (partially similar):** similarity in $[0.3, 0.8]$; same task type with different objects or entities.
- **L2 (near-duplicate and exact repeat):** similarity above 0.8, from same-parameter different-trial variants up to verbatim repetition.
- **L3 (compositional):** every typed component of the task — for ALFWorld the tuple (template, object, receptacle, scene); for multi-hop QA the hop chain $\langle (e_1, r_1), (e_2, r_2), \ldots \rangle$ of entities and relations with their gold documents — has appeared individually in the seed history, but the specific combination has not. Candidates are sampled from the Cartesian product of seen component values, filtered for feasibility (for 2WikiMultihopQA, the dataset's (subject, relation, object) evidence triples make compositional validity programmatically checkable), and each L3 item records the seed tasks its parts came from.
- **L4 (spuriously similar):** a minimal edit $\varepsilon$ applied to a seed task $x$ with stored solution $\pi^*$, admitted if and only if (i) $\text{sim}(x', x) \ge \tau$, where $\tau$ is the median similarity of L2 duplicate pairs in that domain — so an L4 item looks like a repeat to the retriever — and (ii) mechanically replaying $\pi^*$ on $x'$ fails under the final scorer. Condition (ii) is verified by exhaustive replay for every admitted item, guaranteeing that negative-transfer readings on L4 are not contaminated by traps the old solution could in fact survive. Similarity is measured as embedding cosine over the task instruction (plus the first observation frame) [TODO: pin the embedding model for $\text{sim}(\cdot,\cdot)$; the L4 admission threshold $\tau$ is undefined until this instrument is fixed and declared].

L0–L2 form an honest similarity axis; L3 and L4 are structured probes (compositional generalization and misuse resistance, respectively) and are not claimed to lie on the same monotone axis. Each L4 item is further assigned a trap tier by *divergence depth*: replaying $\pi^*$ on $x'$, let $d^* \in [0,1]$ be the fraction of the trajectory executed before the first contradiction signal (environment error, observation mismatch, or retrieved document lacking the target entity). Tier T1 ($d^* < 0.25$) fails openly at the start; T2 ($0.25 \le d^* < 1$) wastes a prefix before surfacing; T3 (no contradiction signal at all, failure visible only at final scoring) is the silent-error tier. Released L4 sets are balanced across T1–T3 and carry tier labels in metadata.

**L4 probe-stream design.** An L4 stream consists of a *seeding* segment of L2 episodes — with a minimum-success gate; runs whose seeding success falls below the gate are discarded wholesale, since negative transfer is undefined when nothing was memorized [TODO: set the seeding success threshold] — followed by a *probe* segment of L4 items during which memory writes are frozen and retrieval is read-only. The nomem arm traverses the identical stream, including the seeding segment, so that position and context length are matched.

## Metrics

Let $P_k$ denote the paired grid at episode index $k$: the set of runs in which both arms completed all $K$ episodes and both arms have status ok at $(r, k)$.

**Speedup@k.**
$$\mathrm{Sp}@k \;=\; 1 - \frac{\sum_{r \in P_k} c^{\text{mem}}_{r,k}}{\sum_{r \in P_k} c^{\text{nomem}}_{r,k}}.$$
The denominator pairs each episode against the memory-off floor on the same task instance, seed, and stream position, cancelling positional difficulty. We use a ratio of sums rather than a mean of per-run ratios because per-episode token savings are heavy-tailed (near-zero medians with large means in our pilots), and per-run ratios explode on small denominators; the ratio of sums is token-weighted and stable. Uncertainty is estimated by bootstrap over runs. Two reporting rules are mandatory: if $|P_k| < m$ (we use $m = 4$) the point is omitted — plotted as a gap, never filled with surviving runs — and $\mathrm{Sp}@k$ never appears without the accompanying $\mathrm{Acc}@k = \frac{1}{|P_k|} \sum_{r \in P_k} u^{\text{mem}}_{r,k}$ on the same grid. Speed alone is gameable: in our injection pilot, an arm fed erroneous documents *saved* 319 tokens per episode while soft accuracy fell by 34 points [TODO: cross-reference the pilot section reporting this number].

**Cost-to-competence.** Define the windowed competence $\hat{p}_{r,k} = \frac{1}{w} \sum_{j=k-w+1}^{k} u_{r,j}$ with $w = 3$, and the attainment index $k^*_r(\theta) = \min \{ k \ge w : \hat{p}_{r,k} \ge \theta \}$, with $k^*_r = \infty$ if the threshold is never reached (censored). Then
$$\mathrm{CTC}_r(\theta) \;=\; \sum_{j=1}^{\min(k^*_r,\, K)} c_{r,j}.$$
The sum includes failed episodes: tuition includes failed attempts, which is precisely what distinguishes this metric from terminal accuracy. Censored runs are charged the full-stream budget $\sum_{j=1}^{K} c_{r,j}$ and reported with a $\ge$ qualifier, always alongside the attainment rate $\rho(\theta) = |\{ r : k^*_r \le K \}| / |R|$. Averaging over attaining runs only is prohibited. Summary statistic: median, which is robust to censored values. We report $\theta$ on two tracks — absolute ($\theta \in \{0.5, 0.7\}$) and relative ($\theta$ = the nomem full-stream mean $+\,10$ points) — because on hard domains the absolute track can be uniformly censored while the relative track remains comparable across domains.

**Negative-transfer index.** On the L4 probe segment $Q$ (seeding episodes excluded), over the ok-only paired grid:
$$\mathrm{NTI}_{\text{acc}} \;=\; \bar{u}^{\text{nomem}}_Q - \bar{u}^{\text{mem}}_Q, \qquad \mathrm{NTI}_{\text{cost}} \;=\; \frac{\bar{c}^{\text{mem}}_Q - \bar{c}^{\text{nomem}}_Q}{\bar{c}^{\text{nomem}}_Q},$$
where $\bar{u}_Q$ and $\bar{c}_Q$ are means over $Q$. Positive values indicate negative transfer on either axis; signs are preserved rather than clipped, since a memory system that profits on traps is itself an informative reading. We deliberately do not combine the two into a single scalar: $(\mathrm{NTI}_{\text{acc}} > 0, \mathrm{NTI}_{\text{cost}} < 0)$ — faster but wrong, the T3 signature — and $(\mathrm{NTI}_{\text{acc}} > 0, \mathrm{NTI}_{\text{cost}} > 0)$ — slower and wrong, the T2 signature — are distinct failure modes that a weighted sum would average away. Results are reported as two-dimensional points stratified by trap tier, with at least $n \ge 10$ probe items per tier for a tier to be reported separately. Two companion readings contextualize NTI. First, the *mistransfer rate* $\mathrm{MTR}$: the fraction of probe episodes whose action prefix copies the stored solution beyond its divergence depth $d^*$. A significant $\mathrm{NTI}_{\text{acc}}$ with $\mathrm{MTR} \approx 0$ must not be attributed to experience misuse — the drop has another cause (e.g., context inflation), and the nomem arm on the same items supplies the task-difficulty control. Second, the same subject's delta on L0 streams should be $\approx 0$; a drop on L0 indicates indiscriminate mechanism overhead and is attributed separately from targeted misuse on L4.

## Aggregation Rules Against Survivorship Bias

All metrics obey five rules, implemented as assertions in the analysis code rather than as conventions:

1. **Complete-grid rule.** Per-episode curves use only runs that completed all $K$ episodes; partial runs never enter positional means.
2. **Paired-difference rule.** Every mem$-$nomem comparison is made on the grid aligned by (run, episode index, task instance); if either arm is missing a cell, the cell is dropped for both.
3. **Three-status audit rule.** Headline metrics are ok-only, but the ok rate of every condition is itself reported; a condition that inflates its ok-only mean by shedding hard episodes is exposed by its ok rate.
4. **Explicit-censoring rule.** Censored CTC values are charged the full budget, carry a $\ge$ qualifier, and are accompanied by the attainment rate; attainers-only averages are forbidden.
5. **Hardware-stratification rule.** Wall-clock times are never pooled across hardware; cross-machine comparisons use tokens only.

These rules are not hypothetical safeguards: an early pilot computed positional means over an incomplete matrix in which fast seeds finished first, producing a spurious 63% speedup that vanished under the complete-grid rule.

## Ecological Validity

A benchmark built on streams of similar tasks is only meaningful if such streams dominate real workloads. We measure this on a real coding-agent trace corpus (357k turns, 4,265 sessions, 3,594 usable) [TODO: add TraceLab / syfi_coding_trace citation and licensing details]. Session-to-session similarity is computed as the cosine between tool-invocation count vectors, since trace content is redacted; this instrument captures tool-composition similarity only, a boundary we state explicitly. Three findings ground the design. First, *repetition is the bulk of the workload*: adjacent-session similarity has a median of 0.93, with 68.4% of adjacent pairs above 0.8 (the L2 regime), 17.2% in $[0.3, 0.8]$ (L1), and 14.5% below 0.3 (L0). Second, *similar tasks arrive in tight clusters*: 96.1% of sessions have a predecessor with similarity above 0.8 within a 50-session window, at a median gap of 1 session (90th percentile: 4) — so memory and prefetching need only a short shelf life to capture most of the available reuse. Third, *spurious similarity occurs naturally*: 10.8% of high-similarity pairs span different projects, i.e., tasks that look alike to a retriever but live in different contexts. The L4 probe is thus a controlled-dose version of a failure mode that already exists in production traffic, not an adversarial construction.

## Subjects and Baselines

**Baselines** bound the achievable range on every stream:

- **Memory-off**: the paired nomem arm; the floor that all speedups are measured against.
- **Oracle replay**: an upper reference that is handed the stored solution of the most similar prior task [TODO: finalize the oracle's retrieval rule and whether it replays verbatim or conditions on the trace].
- **Long-context**: the full interaction history placed in context with no external memory mechanism — a baseline that prior evaluations warn is strong.
- **Shotgun prefetch**: speculatively fires the top-$k$ most likely tool calls at episode start; a learned speculative policy must beat this unlearned saturation strategy [TODO: shotgun requires content prediction and is blocked on the parameter-head revision; not yet run at any scale].

**Subjects** are memory-augmented agents evaluated under the frozen-backbone protocol.
